Hostel Rules & Regulations

We maintain a structured and disciplined ecosystem for everyone's safety.

  • Entry gate curfew timings must be strictly followed (Evening restriction by 8:00 PM).
  • Visitors and family members are permitted exclusively in the reception area during visiting hours.
  • Maintaining basic cleanliness and hygiene within personal rooms and common areas is mandatory.
  • Strictly zero-tolerance policy for smoking, alcohol consumption, or prohibited substances on the premises.
  • A prior notice of at least 15 days is mandatory before vacating your room.
  • Lights and fans must be turned off when leaving the room to ensure efficient energy conservation.
